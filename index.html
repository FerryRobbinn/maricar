Meet the Owner

Bongabon,
let's share a cup.

Growing up in the province of Bongabon, I always loved the rich aroma of coffee beans. But after spending years working and studying in the city — where coffee was often expensive — hindi masarap and it lost its comforting feeling.

More than anything, I wanted to create a cozy neighborhood space named after my beloved cartoon, Sofia the First, and my favorite character, Prince James — naming the shop Prince.

My mission was simple: bridge the gap between aesthetic café culture and accessible, authentic Filipino comfort. I opened Prince Coffee to prove that a great cup of local Barako, a warm pan de sal, or a creamy tsokolate didn't have to cost a lot.

- Maricar
